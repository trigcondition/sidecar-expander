:::caution[Not in 0.5.0]
The MORPH page, its scenes and its fader are **hidden in Sidecar 0.5.0**. They come in **0.5.1**, together with a new version of morph, the looper, sidechain and the learn rows on the MIDI page.
:::

In 0.5.0:

- **UP + T14** is the Rytm's own key. The SIDECAR MENU has no MORPH row, and the touchscreen's Settings > Send FX has no Morph key.
- **MORPH is not a target** of the XY pad or an LFO.
- **A project that already uses the Sidecar's morph keeps its settings.** The scenes stay in the project's files on the card (`/.rytm/scene/`) and are not changed or deleted. They work again when 0.5.1 is installed.

This page stays so that links to it keep working. It is not in the sidebar.
