Choose the board, the display and the settings; the card's `config.txt` and `cmdline.txt` change as you go. Copy them or download them, and put them at the top level of the Sidecar SD card in place of the card's own (unzip the [release card](/sidecar-expander/getting-started/install-pi/#make-the-sd-card) first). With nothing changed you get the 0.4.4 card's files exactly. Everything runs in this page: nothing is sent, nothing is stored, and the page works offline.

<ConfigEditor />

Every option here is one the Sidecar 0.4.4 firmware reads; the [install page](/sidecar-expander/getting-started/install-pi/#options-cmdlinetxt) and [The display](/sidecar-expander/using/display/) explain them in more detail. A setting saved on the unit itself (Settings on the touchscreen, the SIDECAR menu on the Rytm) can win over `cmdline.txt`: the USB-C mode, for example, follows the saved one.
