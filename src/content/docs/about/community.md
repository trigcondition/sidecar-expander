Sidecar has a **Discord server**, run and moderated by its community. It's the place to:

- ask for help with installing the Pi, screens and cables, and flashing the mods;
- share tracks, kits, videos and user machines you made;
- discuss feature ideas and the roadmap;
- follow and help with porting Sidecar to other Elektron boxes.

## Bugs and tester reports

Discord is good for a quick question. For a bug that needs fixing, also file a **Tester report** under [Issues](https://github.com/trigcondition/sidecar-expander/issues/new/choose), with your logs, so it doesn't get lost in the chat ([Reporting](/sidecar-expander/using/troubleshooting/#reporting)).

Never post your web page's pairing code, your Wi-Fi password or a patched firmware file, on Discord or anywhere else.

The Discord is a community space. Like Sidecar itself, it is not made, endorsed or supported by Elektron.
