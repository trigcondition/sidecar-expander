Sound

Output and FIFO are the USB host build's; the serial build answers them with a note.

USB

Audio mode needs a Sidecar | Expander (Mini) build (teensy/host_uac2.sh). A change re-enumerates the Teensy (the page reconnects by itself); the Rytm keeps streaming. The mode is kept across reboots.

Browse the SD card

USB disk mode (the card as a disk on the computer, faster than the browser) is not in this firmware yet. When it comes, the computer's audio and MIDI to the Teensy stop until you switch back. The Rytm side keeps running. Until then, use the card browser (Files).
