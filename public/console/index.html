Sound

Never send channels 10/11 back into the Rytm through Overbridge (computer audio, a mixer loop): that is a feedback loop.

Output and FIFO are the USB host build's; the serial build answers them with a note.

LFOs

Four LFOs on the Sidecar's voices (V1..V8: any parameter) or its send FX (FX1 / FX2 P2..P5), on top of the Rytm's value; the value comes back when the LFO stops. Saved per kit, as the Rytm's own LFOs (switched with the Rytm's kit); the same on the Rytm's LFO page (TRK + YES).

Record

0:00:00
_001, _002, ... in /Recordings

Recording without PSRAM needs a fast SD card (A1/A2, U3/V30 class): the Teensy's own buffer holds only 57 ms of 24-bit stereo (85 ms at 16-bit). On a slower card, or if "the card fell behind" shows, record 16-bit.

Levels

The peak of the last half second while armed or recording, on the channels the take records. One WAV a channel (MASTER as a stereo file), all starting on the same frame and the same length, so they line up in a DAW; the same layout as the Sidecar (Pi). The takes are in Files, /Recordings.

USB

Stereo: the Sidecar Mini is also a 2 in / 2 out USB audio interface on this port, 48 kHz. The computer records the Rytm's MAIN L/R (with the Sidecar's voices as the Rytm plays them), and what it plays goes to the Rytm's MAIN input. A change re-enumerates the Teensy (the page reconnects by itself); the Rytm keeps streaming. The mode is kept across reboots. Multi (20 in / 12 out + MIDI) is the teensy/host_uac2.sh build.
