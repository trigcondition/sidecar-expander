JUNO/Factory/*.syx
  The Roland Juno-106's 128 factory patch settings, as published in AMY
  (shorepine/amy, amy/juno.py), written as Juno-106 patch dumps. The same data
  is built into the Sidecar firmware's JUNO machine.
  AMY is MIT licensed: Copyright (c) Brian Whitman, Dan Ellis.

DX7/*.syx
  The three 32-voice banks of DX7 voices that Mutable Instruments Plaits
  carries in plaits/resources.cc (Emilie Gillet's selection of DX7 patches,
  "My favorite DX7 patches", from DX7 ROM and other classic banks), written as
  standard DX7 bank dumps. The same data is built into the Sidecar firmware's
  6-OP FM machine. Mutable Instruments eurorack code is MIT licensed:
  Copyright 2016 Emilie Gillet.

"Juno-106" and "DX7" are trademarks of their owners and are used only to say
what the files work with.
